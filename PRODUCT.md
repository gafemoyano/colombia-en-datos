# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Public analysts, researchers, and other people exploring Colombian demographic, economic, and statistical indicators. Internal technical data operators administer indicator definitions and multi-indicator Parquet batches; they understand indicator and frequency codes, dimensions, canonical schemas, validation, staging, and publication concepts.

## Product Purpose

Colombia en Datos makes Colombian statistical indicators understandable and explorable while preserving trustworthy ingestion, canonical observations, lineage, and publication controls. Success means users can rely on what they see, and administrators can move data from source delivery to publication through an explicit, auditable, and recoverable workflow.

## Brand Personality

Sober, precise, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a decorative SaaS onboarding wizard. Avoid oversized generic cards, ornamental progress effects, hidden technical detail, ambiguous partial saves, and friendly-sounding copy that obscures validation failures, replacement semantics, lineage, or publication consequences.

## Design Principles

1. Put data truth and operational clarity ahead of decoration.
2. Use statistical and ingestion domain language precisely rather than diluting it for technical operators.
3. Make workflow state, validation evidence, and destructive consequences explicit before actions occur.
4. Prefer atomic, recoverable operations with actionable retry paths over optimistic partial progress.
5. Preserve provenance and auditability from source batch through every published slice.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, strongly visible focus, screen-reader announcements for errors and workflow status, status communication that does not rely on color alone, accessible tables and labels, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion preferences.
